How the player rating is calculated

MatchGrade turns a match stat line into a single rating out of 10, using the same starting point professional rating services use: a 6.0 baseline represents an anonymous, did-their-job performance. From there, the score moves up or down based on how each stat compares to what's typical for that specific position or role a center-back's rating leans on tackles and clearances, a striker's leans on goals and shot quality, a point guard's leans on assists and turnovers, and a bowler's leans on wickets and economy rather than runs scored.

Every sport has its own scoring model, because the same raw number means something different depending on the role. Ten tackles is an outstanding game for a full-back and an odd stat line for a striker. MatchGrade only asks for the numbers that role actually generates, and shows exactly which stats pushed the rating up or down so the score is never a black box.

Soccer ratings

Choose from goalkeeper, centre-back, full-back, defensive mid, central mid, attacking mid, winger, or striker. Core stats are tailored to the role, with optional extras like dribbles, aerial duels, and errors leading to a goal for a more precise score.

Basketball ratings

Choose point guard, shooting guard, small forward, power forward, or center. The rating weighs shooting efficiency, rebounding, assists, and turnovers differently by position, with optional plus-minus and steal/block stats available for a sharper number.

Cricket ratings

Choose batter, bowler, all-rounder, or wicket-keeper. Batting rating factors in runs, balls faced, and strike rate; bowling factors in overs, wickets, and economy rate; wicket-keepers get credit for catches and stumpings on top of their batting line.

Frequently asked questions

What is MatchGrade?

MatchGrade is a free player rating calculator that turns match statistics into an honest rating out of 10 for soccer, basketball, and cricket. Enter the numbers from a game and get a transparent score based on position or role.

How does the MatchGrade player rating calculator work?

Every rating starts at a 6.0 baseline, which represents an anonymous, did-their-job performance. The score then moves up or down depending on how each stat compares to what is typical for that specific position or role. The tool shows exactly which stats pushed the rating higher or lower so nothing is a black box.

Is MatchGrade free to use?

Yes. MatchGrade is completely free. There are no accounts, subscriptions, or hidden fees. Enter stats, get a rating, and share the card if you want.

Which sports does MatchGrade support?

MatchGrade currently supports three sports: soccer (football), basketball, and cricket. Each sport has its own position- or role-specific scoring model so the same raw number is interpreted differently depending on the role.

Why does every rating start at 6.0?

A 6.0 baseline is the same starting point used by many professional rating services. It represents a neutral, anonymous performance where the player did their job without standing out positively or negatively. Everything above or below that is earned or lost from the stats.

Do different positions use different weighting?

Yes. A center-back is judged more on tackles, interceptions, clearances and aerials. A striker is judged more on goals, shot conversion and touches in the box. A point guard leans on assists and turnovers. A bowler is scored on wickets and economy. MatchGrade only asks for the stats that role actually produces.

Can I rate players from any league or level?

Yes. MatchGrade works for any level — professional, amateur, youth, or recreational. The calculator does not require a specific league or competition. Just enter the stats from the match.

What stats do I need for a soccer player rating?

Core stats depend on position. Goalkeepers need saves and shots faced. Defenders need tackles, interceptions and clearances. Midfielders and attackers need key passes, goals, assists and dribbles. Optional extras such as duels, aerials, and errors leading to a goal can refine the score further.

How are basketball ratings calculated?

Basketball ratings start at 6.0 and adjust based on points, shooting percentages, rebounds, assists, turnovers, steals, blocks and plus-minus. Each position (point guard, shooting guard, small forward, power forward, center) weights these stats differently to reflect typical contributions.

How do cricket ratings work on MatchGrade?

Choose batter, bowler, all-rounder or wicket-keeper. Batting factors in runs, balls faced and strike rate. Bowling factors in overs, wickets and economy. Wicket-keepers also receive credit for catches and stumpings. Optional stats such as boundaries, maidens and byes can be added for more precision.

Can I share my MatchGrade rating?

Yes. After grading a performance you can open a shareable rating card. The card can be saved as an image or shared directly from supported devices. It includes the player name, position, rating, verdict and key contributing stats.

Is MatchGrade the same as WhoScored or SofaScore ratings?

MatchGrade uses a similar 6.0 baseline philosophy but is an independent tool. It is designed to be transparent: every rating shows exactly which stats moved the score up or down. It is not affiliated with any major data provider and works from the numbers you enter yourself.

Do I need an account to use MatchGrade?

No. MatchGrade is a free, no-login tool. Open the page, select the sport, enter the stats and get the rating instantly. Nothing is stored on a server unless you choose to share the rating card.

Does playing time affect the rating?

Yes. Soccer and basketball ratings scale stats to a full-match equivalent (90 minutes or 32 minutes) so short appearances are not unfairly punished or rewarded. Very limited minutes also gently pull the final rating toward the neutral 6.0 baseline.
